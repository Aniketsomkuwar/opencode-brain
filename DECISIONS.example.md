# Decisions

Durable technical and product decisions. Append only. Never rewrite or delete an entry; supersede it with a new one.

Each entry answers: what was decided, why, and what it replaces.

## Format

```markdown
### YYYY-MM-DD - <SHORT TITLE>

- Decision: <WHAT WAS DECIDED>
- Why: <THE CONSTRAINT OR REASON>
- Supersedes: <EARLIER DECISION, OR "none">
```
